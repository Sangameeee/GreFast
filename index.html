The Architecture

Why We Built This Website

Excelling in the GRE Verbal Reasoning section requires two synchronized disciplines: an expansive high-frequency vocabulary and the ability to dissect sentence logic under strict timed constraints.

🏛️

The Big Book Foundation

The ETS GRE Big Book contains 27 authentic published exams. While the test format has evolved, Big Book sentence completion questions remain the gold standard for mastering ETS trap structures, contrast pivots, support clues, and complex double-blank coordination.

🧠

GregMat's 34 Groups

Spanning over 1,020 vetted vocabulary words, GregMat's 34 Groups are universally recognized as the most effective high-yield vocabulary syllabus. Each word is paired with clear definitions, parts of speech, and illustrative context sentences.

🔄

Unified Feedback Loop

When practicing Big Book TC questions, you inevitably discover unfamiliar words. Instead of writing them down in fragmented notebooks, you can immediately record them into the Custom Big Book deck in the Vocabulary module and rate them for review.

Proven Study Roadmap

The 4-Step Mastery Flywheel

Maximize score improvements by alternating between active vocabulary acquisition and sentence completion logic drills.

1

Learn & Rate Words

Open Vocabulary Master (Misc). Study 1 group (approx. 30 words). Flip cards with Space and rate each word: 1 for mastered, 2 for review, 3 for hard.

2

Targeted Rated Drill

Switch to the Practice Rated tab. Drill specifically on words rated 2 and 3 until your confidence transitions them to 1. Stop wasting time on words you already know.

3

Prediction TC Practice

Navigate to Text Completion (TC). Choose a section in Practice Prediction Mode. Fill your own predictions into the blanks before revealing answer choices.

4

Rapid Timed Simulation

Re-attempt completed tests in Rapid Timed Mode with a 45s or 60s countdown to build effortless intuition, high speed, and real exam stamina.

Questions & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my study data and progress saved?

All your data is saved directly in your PC! Your quiz attempts, test scores, section completion statuses, and flashcard confidence ratings are stored securely in your browser's local storage—no account or login required.

Can I use this on a mobile device or tablet?

Absolutely. All pages feature fully responsive layouts, touch gestures, mobile-friendly cards, and collapsible menus.

What makes Practice Prediction Mode effective?

Scientific testing shows that guessing what word belongs in the blank before viewing multiple-choice distractors prevents confirmation bias and trains genuine verbal reasoning.
